Did You Know!

Bananas are nutritious, potassium-rich berries that grow on large herbs, not trees, and are known for boosting mood and heart health due to tryptophan and potassium. They are a staple crop, with over 100 billion eaten yearly, and surprisingly, humans share 50% of their DNA with them, while banana peels have various uses, from polishing shoes to natural fertilizer.
